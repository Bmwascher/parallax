## What these records establish, and what they do not

**They establish** that rounds 5 and 6 of the plan debate each blocked
the merge on a finding the session then fixed, and that only round 7 was
terminal. A flat cap of four exchanges would have stopped this debate
two rounds before its last two real defects were found. That is backlog
item 24's case, and it is now readable rather than remembered.

**They do not establish** that no point was ever contested. The replies
record verdicts per claim, not an argued-across-rounds tally, and no
round record carries a contested-point count. A reader reconstructing
that has to read the claims themselves. The 0.20.0 `6,5,2,1,0` sequence
is still not retained anywhere and stays an assertion.

Every reply is verbatim as the reviewer returned it, including its own
UNVERIFIED section, which is the part that says what that round did NOT
check. The absolute repo paths inside the citations are the reviewer's
own; they are left as written, because editing a retained record to look
tidier is the one thing a retained record may not be.
